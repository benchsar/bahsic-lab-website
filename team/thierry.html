Dr. Thierry Peyrard, PharmD, PhD, HDR, European Specialist in Laboratory Medicine, is a director of research in the BAHSic laboratory, working in the field of immunohematology and blood transfusion. His research focuses on the characterization of new blood group systems and red cell antigens, and to investigate the role of blood group-carrying molecules in mature red cells and erythropoiesis.

Research interests

  • Erythroid blood group systems
  • Red cell antigens
  • Rare blood types
  • Transfusion safety in patients with sickle cell disease

Key expertise

  • Serological and molecular investigation of complex cases in immunohematology
  • Blood group genotyping
  • Blood products for therapeutic use
  • Transfusion safety and transfusion-associated adverse events

Background

  • HDR “Hematology and Oncology” – University Paris Descartes, Paris (2013)
  • PhD in “Epidemiology and Biomedical Information Sciences” – University Pierre & Marie Curie, Paris (2011)
  • University Degree in "Blood Transfusion Medicine", University Pierre & Marie Curie, Paris (1997)
  • Pharmacy Doctoral Degree, University of Pharmacy, Marseille (1997)
  • MSc in "Molecular and Cellular Immunobiology”, University of Science and Technology, Montpellier (1993)
