Bérengère KOEHL is a Pediatrician, Professor of Medicine and Hospital Practitioner in the Sickle Cell Disease Centre at Robert Debré Hospital in Paris and co-head of the team BaHSic. Her research focuses hematopoiesis in Sickle Cell Disease, vaso-occlusion cellular processes, chronic inflammation in SCD and transfusion medicine.

Research interests

  • Sickle Cell Disease
  • Hematopoiesis
  • Transfusion Medicine

Background

  • PhD in Cellular Biology – University of Paris Cité (2017)
  • MD, PhD of Pediatrics- University of Paris Diderot (2011)
  • Master in Proteomics– University of Paris Diderot (2009)

Current projects

  • Investigating the impact of inflammatory signaling on hematopoietic stem cell differentiation using single-cell transcriptomics and functional assays.
  • Elucidating the cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying vaso-occlusive events in Sickle Cell Disease, as well as potential therapeutic reversal strategies.
  • Deciphering the pathophysiological mechanisms of severe transfusion-related adverse events.
