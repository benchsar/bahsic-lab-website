Frédéric Martino

Assistant professor

Inserm U1134 – BAHSic Lab · Faculty of Medicine, Université des Antilles

✉️ Email ← Back to team

Dr Frédéric Martino is an Associate Professor in Intensive Care Medicine at the University Hospital of Guadeloupe and a researcher at Inserm U1134 (BAHSic Lab). His work focuses on sepsis, inflammation, and platelet activation, particularly in tropical and critical care settings. He is also actively involved in medical education and simulation-based training.

Research interests

  • Sepsis and systemic inflammation
  • Platelet activation and inflammasome pathways
  • Tropical infectious diseases (leptospirosis, COVID-19)
  • Critical care in resource-limited and insular settings
  • Medical simulation and education

Key expertise

  • Sepsis and critical illness research
  • Clinical research and multicenter studies
  • Flow cytometry

Background

  • PhD student in Biological Sciences – University of the French West Indies (since 2020)
  • MSc in Tropical Health and Biology – University of the French West Indies (2020)
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Emergency Medicine (2013)
  • Medical Degree (General Medicine) – University of the French West Indies (2012)

Current projects

Investigating the role of inflammasome activation in platelet function during bacterial and viral sepsis. Research on critical care management in isolated territories, including medical evacuation and severe neurological conditions. Development of simulation-based training programs for undergraduate and postgraduate medical education.
