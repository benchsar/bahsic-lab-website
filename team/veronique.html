Véronique is a researcher in the BAHSic laboratory, his project aims to study platelet activation mainly in sickle cell disease but also in another inflammatory diseases like sepsis. Her study focuses on research the signaling pathways involved in platelet activation.

Current projects

Studying of platelet signaling pathways involved in the activation of platelets in sickle cell disease and other inflammatory diseases.

Investigating platelet activation and reactivity profiles and different thrombin generation profiles in SS and SC patients.

Studying of the different lymphoid subpopulations in sickle cell disease.
