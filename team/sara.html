Sara is a bioinformatician in the BAHSic laboratory with broad expertise in high-throughput data analysis and computational biology. Her work spans bulk RNA-seq, single-cell transcriptomics, and exome sequencing, with a strong focus on extracting biologically meaningful insights from complex datasets. She also develops interactive tools and web interfaces to facilitate data exploration and scientific communication.

Research interests

  • Hematopoiesis and red blood cell biology
  • Single-cell and bulk transcriptomics
  • Genomic variation and exome analysis
  • Data integration and multi-omics approaches
  • Translational bioinformatics in hematological diseases

Key expertise

  • RNA-seq analysis (bulk and single-cell)
  • Exome sequencing analysis (variant calling & interpretation)
  • Interactive data visualization (R Shiny applications)
  • Web development (HTML, CSS)
  • Reproducible workflows (R, Python, HPC environments)

Background

  • Master’s Degree in Bioinformatics, Modeling, and Statistics

  • MBachelor’s Degree in Cellular and Molecular Biology

Current projects

Analysis of single-cell RNA-seq datasets to characterize cellular heterogeneity and identify disease-associated transcriptional signatures. Development of robust pipelines for data integration, clustering, and annotation, as well as statistical analyses to support biological interpretation.
